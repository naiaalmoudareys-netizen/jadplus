# JAD+ record of personal data processing (UAE PDPL)

Draft for JAD legal counsel. Started 09-Nov-2026.

| Data                     | Whose                               | Why (purpose)                                | Where stored                                                                                                                                                                                               | Kept for                                                         | Who can see                                                                        |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Name, email, mobile      | Owners, tenants                     | Sign-in, service updates                     | Salesforce (owners; JAD+ keeps an encrypted copy taken at sign-in, ADR 0005); JAD+ D1 encrypted (tenants)                                                                                                  | While account active + 1 year                                    | The person; JAD+ Admin; the partner assigned to their request (name + mobile only) |
| Unit, deal, payment plan | Owners                              | Show their residence and payments            | Salesforce (read live and passed to the owner app, not stored: ADR 0010); JAD+ copies only unit id, deal id, unit and project name (owner_units, ADR 0005)                                                 | Unit list replaced at each sign-in; removed when no longer owner | The owner                                                                          |
| Emirates ID / passport   | Owners, tenants (lockout check E15) | Identity verification                        | R2 private, encrypted; owners' own copies stay in Salesforce and are shown to that owner on request in the app (ADR 0010: fetched per view, temporary copy on the phone deleted on next view and sign-out) | While needed + legal minimum                                     | JAD+ Admin; technician sees only "verified"                                        |
| Request details, photos  | Owners, tenants                     | Deliver the service                          | D1, R2 private (photos re-saved on the phone and stripped of location / camera metadata by the API, ADR 0013)                                                                                              | 5 years (warranty/disputes) — confirm with legal                 | Requester, owner, assigned partner, JAD+ Admin                                     |
| Ratings, comments        | Owners, tenants                     | Partner scorecards (internal)                | D1                                                                                                                                                                                                         | 3 years                                                          | JAD+ Admin                                                                         |
| Consent records          | All users                           | Prove terms/marketing consent (L1)           | D1                                                                                                                                                                                                         | 5 years after account closes                                     | JAD+ Admin, legal                                                                  |
| Offer interest           | Owners                              | Sales follow-up on an owners-only offer (N2) | D1 (offer_interests: owner id, offer id, time)                                                                                                                                                             | Until handled + 1 year                                           | Owner; JAD+ Admin; sales                                                           |
| Desk request details     | Owners                              | The JAD+ desk acts on the request (ADR 0011) | Salesforce (JAD+ Request); JAD+ log in D1, details encrypted                                                                                                                                               | While account active + 1 year                                    | Owner; JAD+ desk                                                                   |
| Audit log                | Staff and users                     | Security, disputes                           | D1                                                                                                                                                                                                         | 1 year minimum                                                   | Naya, auditor                                                                      |

Rights: access, correction (via "Talk to a person", U2), deletion on account closure (except where law
requires keeping records), marketing opt-out at any time. Breach process: see SECURITY.md section 5.
